# SmartCare AI

AI-powered customer support for small businesses that helps customers ask questions, get product recommendations, and submit complaints while giving business owners a simple dashboard to review and manage follow-up.

## The Unique Kernel
The distinctive part of SmartCare AI is that it combines customer support and complaint handling in one workflow: a customer asks a question, receives a grounded response from the business data, gets a product recommendation, and can raise a complaint that gets tracked for business follow-up. This is not just a chatbot mock-up; it is a practical support loop that connects the customer experience to the business owner’s dashboard.

## Who It's For
Small businesses, especially online retailers and local entrepreneurs, that do not have a full customer support team and need a simple, affordable way to handle routine customer questions and complaints.

Their current reality is that they answer questions manually, often too slowly, and risk missing complaints that require attention. SmartCare AI helps them handle those tasks in a more organized, faster, and more consistent way.

## The Core Loop
A customer opens the chat, asks about a product or business service, and gets a useful answer based on sample business information and a product catalogue. They can then describe their need and receive a product recommendation with a short explanation. If a problem occurs, they can file a complaint, and the system records it as a follow-up item for the owner. The owner reviews the enquiry or complaint in a dashboard, sees status and context, and updates the case as needed.

## Inspiration & Identity
The product should feel professional, warm, and trustworthy rather than overly playful or generic. It should feel like a polished business tool for a small company: clean, mobile-friendly, simple to navigate, and grounded in the real problems a small business faces when handling customer support.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
This project matters because it combines useful AI functionality with practical business value. It is a realistic demonstration of how AI can reduce operational overhead, improve customer experience, and ensure that complaint issues are not ignored. It also fits the hackathon goal of showing an end-to-end system that is simple enough to build well within a short timeframe.

## What "Working" Looks Like
A judge can open the app, simulate a customer conversation, ask a product question, receive a grounded answer, request a recommendation, submit a complaint, and then see that complaint appear in the business dashboard with the customer message, status, and follow-up information intact after refresh or navigation. The application demonstrates a full support journey from the customer side to the owner side, using sample data rather than a mock-only experience.

## The POC Boundary
In scope:
- A simple customer-facing chat interface
- Sample business information and product catalogue
- Product recommendation logic based on customer needs
- Complaint submission and tracking
- A business dashboard showing enquiries and complaint status
- Status updates for complaints
- Mobile-friendly, single-business demo setup

Out of scope for the first version:
- Real user accounts or login system
- Payments, fulfilment, or shipping workflow
- Real customer service integrations like WhatsApp or email
- Multi-business accounts or analytics dashboards
- Full AI model tuning or production-grade customer support escalation

## Later
- WhatsApp or messaging integrations
- Multiple business accounts and tenant management
- Custom AI responses by business category
- Complaint analytics and reporting
- Customer support automation rules and workflows

## Explicitly Cut
- Real business authentication and payment processing
- Live order management or inventory system
- Complex CRM or ERP integrations
- Advanced analytics dashboards before the core workflow is working
- Multi-language support or large-scale deployment during the hackathon phase
